# Sweet Kandy

## Register
Brand. The site is the identity: a UK sweets house selling halal mixed pouches.

## Product Purpose
Help people order pouches of mixed sweets (classic, sour, American, or a custom scoop) with clear halal confidence, local delivery, and nationwide post.

## Users
People buying treats for themselves, family, and gifting. Many want halal sweets that still taste like a proper pick-and-mix counter, not a compromise bag.

## Brand Personality
Cheeky, glossy, scoop-first. Corner-shop energy with packaging that looks like a drop.

## Anti-references
Rainbow lollipop logos, pastel candy-shop templates, circus type, fake luxury chocolate, generic three-card feature grids, “elevate your snacking” copy.

## Strategic Principles
- The pouch is the brand. Show sweets through the window, not cartoon mascots.
- Halal is a fact, not a footnote.
- Sell the mix, not a catalogue of 80 SKUs.
- Orders can start on the site and finish on Instagram, the way the shop already works.
