# Design

## Visual theme
Street-confection CPG. Sugar-paper surfaces, pouch-black product stages, cherry as the committed brand colour. Not a pastel sweet shop. Not an editorial magazine.

## Color
- Sugar paper: `oklch(0.955 0.022 85)`
- Ink: `oklch(0.23 0.02 45)`
- Cherry: `oklch(0.55 0.19 25)`
- Gold: `oklch(0.81 0.13 85)`
- Mint: `oklch(0.64 0.09 165)`
- Pouch: `oklch(0.2 0.015 45)`

## Typography
- Display: Bricolage Grotesque
- Body: Epilogue
- Headlines track tight, body stays warm and readable.

## Components
- Floating pill nav, not an edge-glued bar
- Double-bezel image frames
- Pill CTAs with a nested arrow disc
- Asymmetric product rows, never a three-equal card grid

## Layout
Left-led hero, wide section gaps (`py-24` to `py-40`), mobile collapse to a single column with `px-4`.
